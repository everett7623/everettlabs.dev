## What it solves

Independent publications and knowledge bases need a reading experience that keeps navigation,
long titles, code, and practical reference material clear without depending on a heavy frontend
runtime. Rackora provides that structure as a Halo theme.

## Who it is for

Independent publishers, technical writers, documentation teams, and knowledge-base maintainers
running Halo 2.x sites.

## Why it exists

Many publishing themes prioritize prominent visual media over the article itself. Rackora is built
for low-image technical publishing, with a quiet article stream, controlled tag navigation, and
layouts that remain readable on small screens.

## Key capabilities

- Responsive light, dark, print, and reduced-motion presentation modes
- Eleven Halo route templates plus dedicated About and Documentation templates
- Article table of contents, reading progress, heading anchors, code copying, and related reading
- Configurable site profile, social links, article sidebar, categories, tags, and optional banners
- Route-specific canonical URLs, Open Graph, Twitter Card, and visible content metadata
- Optional integrations through Halo extension points for comments, search, RSS, and friend links

## Engineering notes

Rackora is a GPL-3.0 Halo theme that supports Halo `>= 2.20.0`. Its source uses Thymeleaf
templates, TypeScript, Vite, and Highlight.js language modules. The theme package is built and
validated locally before release, with its version kept in sync across `package.json`, `theme.yaml`,
and the release archive.
